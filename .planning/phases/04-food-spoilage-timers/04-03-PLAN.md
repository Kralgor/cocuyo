<objective>
Replace the Food tab placeholder with the Spanish-first local food timer
experience.

This plan exposes the preset catalog and custom item flow, shows active timer
cards from the lifecycle hook, prompts users to review timers when an outage is
detected, shows restored review state after power returns, and includes the
point-of-use path for enabling food warning notifications without implementing
the scheduler itself.

Covers D-01 through D-18 at the user-facing layer by preserving compact grocery
scope, saved-zone outage semantics, local/offline MMKV behavior, honest stale
copy, quick add/remove/reset flows, outage prompts, restoration reset, and
cautious early warning language.
</objective>
